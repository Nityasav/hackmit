# Delegation of Authority — fictional test policy

Effective 2026-01-01. This is synthetic material for testing only.

## Payment approval

| Amount | Who may approve |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $5,000.00 | Business Manager |
| Above $5,000.00 | Principal, countersigned by the Board Chair |

A payment above the approval limit requires a recorded approval before release.
No payment may be approved by the person who raised the purchase order.

## Purchasing

Every vendor invoice must be matched to a purchase order and a goods receipt
before payment. An invoice with no purchase order is held pending investigation.

## Close

Materiality for escalation is $500.00. Differences below it are noted, not escalated.
